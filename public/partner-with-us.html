About AsuraVerse

Organization Snapshot

AsuraVerse Esports Pvt. Ltd. is a founder-led esports organization focused on competitive gaming, talent development, and long-term ecosystem growth.

Key Highlights

  • • Active competitive BGMI roster
  • • Founder-led and independently operated
  • • Community-driven growth approach
  • • Partnership-focused expansion strategy
  • • Long-term vision for sustainable esports development

AsuraVerse is actively seeking strategic partners who share a long-term vision for the growth of esports in India. Our objective is to create meaningful value for partners while building a sustainable esports ecosystem.
